PUMA Analyzer

Thin-film thickness and optical-constants estimation from transmission spectra

Download for Windows

PUMA Analyzer estimates the thickness and optical constants (n, k) of thin films directly from transmission data, using a physics-based transmittance model combined with robust nonlinear global optimization. Built for researchers, graduate students, and QC technicians who need reliable numbers from a raw transmission spectrum, not just a curve fit.

What it derives

From a fitted n(λ), k(λ), and thickness d, PUMA derives dozens of further physical quantities: optical band gap (Tauc, direct and indirect), Urbach energy, Wemple-DiDomenico dispersion, dielectric function, optical conductivity, and a Kramers-Kronig consistency check, among many others.

See the full README for the equations, real screenshots, a downloadable sample dataset with step-by-step instructions, and the complete results tree.
